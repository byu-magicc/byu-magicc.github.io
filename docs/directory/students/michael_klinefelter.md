## About

Michael is a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Brigham Young University. His research interests include autonomous systems, trajectory-based operations, conflict detection and resolution, and control of multi-agent systems.

## Research

Michael's research focuses on the development of methods for safely coordinating autonomous aircraft in shared airspace. His work on the 4D Flight Path Conflict Detection and Resolution project uses polynomial spline representations of four-dimensional aircraft trajectories to enable continuous-time conflict detection, resolution maneuver generation, and trajectory control. He has also worked on distributed multi-agent coordination and control methods, including cyclic pursuit and formation control on closed paths.
