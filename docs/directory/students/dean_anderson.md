## About

Dean Anderson was a master's student in the Brigham Young University Electrical Engineering department, with Dr. Randy Beard as his graduate advisor. He joined the MAGICC Lab in September 2023, at the beginning of his master's program.

His thesis, [Path Planning and Control for Vertical Takeoff and Landing Aircraft](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/etd/11207/), was submitted in April 2026.

## Research

Dean worked on the eVTOL Quadplane project, focusing on path planning for obstacle avoidance and optimization for flight mode transition. He also worked on modular, repairable 3D-printed airframes and aircraft system identification algorithms.
