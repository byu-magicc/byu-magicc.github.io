## About

Ethan is a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Brigham Young University, advised by Dr. Cammy Peterson. Originally from South Carolina, he earned a BS in Computer Engineering from BYU in 2024 and began working as a research assistant under Dr. Peterson shortly afterward.

His interests include autonomous systems, robotic sensing and localization, SLAM, and ROS 2 programming.

## Research

Ethan's research focuses on detecting and tracking hazardous obstacles and regions for robotic systems. His work includes:

- A geometric extended object tracker that uses support normal constraints and negative sensor information to estimate an object's motion and shape without a predefined geometric prior.
- An estimator that uses expendable agents to locate unknown adversarial launch sites, helping protect high-value vehicles operating nearby.
